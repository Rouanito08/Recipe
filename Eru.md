Ingredients
Eru leaves: 2 to 3 cups (finely sliced, washed, and soaked if dried)

Waterleaves: 4 to 5 cups (washed and chopped; spinach or lamb's lettuce can be used as a substitute if waterleaves aren't available)

Meat & Fish:

Smoked fish or stockfish (washed and deboned)

Beef, cow skin (kanda), or smoked meat (boiled until tender)

Palm oil: 1 to 1.5 cups (generous amount is needed for authentic texture)

Crayfish: 1 cup (ground)

Seasoning: Salt, bouillon cubes, and scotch bonnet peppers (to taste)

Water: About 1 to 2 cups for cooking

Instructions
Prepare the Greens:

If your eru leaves are dried, soak them in warm water to soften them up, then squeeze out the excess water. Finely chop the waterleaves.

Cook the Protein & Base:

In a large pot, add your pre-boiled meat, smoked fish, and/or stockfish along with a little water, salt, and seasoning. Let it simmer for a few minutes to build flavor.

Add the Palm Oil:

Pour in the palm oil and let it heat through with the meat and fish stock for about 3 to 5 minutes.

Add the Waterleaves:

Stir in the chopped waterleaves. Let them cook down for about 3 to 5 minutes until they release their moisture and blend into the oil.

Add the Eru:

Add the shredded eru leaves and ground crayfish to the pot. Stir thoroughly to combine everything.

Simmer:

Lower the heat and let the mixture simmer for about 10 to 15 minutes, stirring occasionally to prevent burning. Add a splash of water if it becomes too dry. (Traditional Eru has a firm, chewy texture, so avoid overcooking it to mush).

Taste and Serve:

Adjust salt and pepper to taste, then remove from heat.
