1. Jollof Rice
Ingredients:

2 cups long-grain parboiled rice
4 large tomatoes (blended) or 1 can of tomato puree
1 onion (chopped, plus extra for frying)
1 red bell pepper (blended)
2-3 cloves garlic (minced)
1 teaspoon ginger (minced)
2 tablespoons tomato paste
1-2 teaspoons curry powder
1 teaspoon thyme
2 bay leaves
2-3 cups chicken or vegetable broth
Salt and pepper to taste
Cooking oil
Instructions:

Rinse the rice and set aside.
In a pot, heat oil, sauté chopped onions until golden.
Add garlic and ginger, cook briefly.
Pour in blended tomatoes, red bell pepper, and tomato paste. Cook until the mixture reduces and the oil starts to separate.
Add curry powder, thyme, bay leaves, salt, and pepper.
Add the rice and stir well to coat with the sauce.
Pour in broth, bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer until rice is cooked (about 20-30 minutes).
Fluff rice with a fork and serve hot.
2. Groundnut Soup (Peanut Soup)
Ingredients:

500g chicken or beef (cut into pieces)
1 cup peanut butter (smooth or chunky)
2 large tomatoes (blended) or tomato puree
1 onion (chopped)
2 cloves garlic (minced)
1 teaspoon ginger (minced)
1-2 hot peppers (optional)
1 teaspoon smoked paprika or chili powder
Salt and pepper to taste
Water or broth
Cooking oil
Instructions:

In a pot, heat oil and sauté onions until soft.
Add garlic and ginger, cook briefly.
Add the meat and brown on all sides.
Add blended tomatoes and cook for about 10 minutes.
Mix peanut butter with some warm water to create a smooth paste.
Stir the peanut butter paste into the pot, add more water/broth to reach desired soup consistency.
Add hot peppers, paprika, salt, and pepper.
Cover and simmer for 40-60 minutes, until meat is tender and soup thickens.
Serve with rice balls, fufu, or plain rice.
